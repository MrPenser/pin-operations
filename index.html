PIN Operations

Automated content publishing and performance analysis.

About

PIN Operations is an experimental content automation system that uses authorized platform APIs to create, manage and evaluate digital content.

The system uses the Pinterest API with its own authenticated business account to publish and manage Pins and boards, retrieve performance analytics, and use measured results to improve future content decisions.
