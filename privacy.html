PIN Operations may use the Pinterest API in accordance with Pinterest's developer terms and applicable platform policies.

Data sharing

PIN Operations does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or platforms when necessary to operate the system, provide analytics, maintain infrastructure, or fulfill legal obligations.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials, API access, and operational data from unauthorized access.

Data retention

Operational and analytics information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for analysis, system integrity, record keeping, security, and legal compliance.

Changes to this policy
